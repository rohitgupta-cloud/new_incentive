ShopDeck Incentive Portal

An internal tool for ShopDeck and Blitzscale employees

ShopDeck Incentive Portal is an internal dashboard used by ShopDeck and Blitzscale employees to view incentive figures calculated from a set of shared internal Google Sheets. Instead of opening several spreadsheets and doing the math by hand, employees sign in with their own Google account and the portal reads the sheets they already have access to and shows the calculated results in one place.

Who this is for

Access is limited to employees with a @shopdeck.com or @blitzscale.co Google account.

How it works

When you sign in, the portal reads only the Google Sheets data your own account already has permission to view — it never grants access beyond what you already have, and it cannot edit or modify any sheet. There is no backend server: everything happens directly in your browser and nothing is stored after your session ends. Full details are in the privacy policy.
